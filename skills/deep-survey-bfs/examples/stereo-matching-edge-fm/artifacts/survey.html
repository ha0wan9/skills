The user's third deliverable: which models suit which data characteristics. Grounded in claims C001-C022:

§10 Open challenges¶

  1. Foundation-VFM × edge deployment — the indexed FM family (FoundationStereo, DEFOM, Stereo Anything, Stereo Anywhere) all run on RTX-class GPUs. No Jetson / mobile port indexed.
  2. TensorRT latency missing (SQ6 confirmed gap). The community does not publish TRT engine numbers in abstracts; they live in repo READMEs and need grep-extraction (R2-H).
  3. Per-distance accuracy breakdowns are rare. Most papers report only aggregate Mean; the few that disaggregate (P022 PPMStereo for temporal, P004 for reflective) are exceptions.
  4. Selective-Stereo's zero-shot regression (Mean 7.28 vs IGEV's 5.51, C015) is unexplained — improving in-domain frequency handling appears to hurt zero-shot generalization. Open question for the field.
  5. Mamba / SSM stereo has only one indexed paper (P020, ICRA 2026). The architectural alternative to Transformer is barely explored.
  6. Synthetic-to-real domain gap is partially addressed by FoundationStereo's 1M synthetic pairs (C011) but is the dominant failure mode per Stereo Anywhere's framing (P021).
  7. Benchmark-adoption inertia tracks institutional concentration (C067). The two top-output labs — HUST (3/18 ★★★) and U Bologna (3/18) — exhibit opposite habits: HUST's papers stick to canonical KITTI/SF/Middlebury/ETH3D; Bologna's introduce emergent datasets (Booster, Stereo Anywhere failure modes). The China-cluster majority (~11/18 ★★★) inherits the canonical-only habit, which means published headline numbers in 2025-2026 still over-index on benchmarks that the field's own critical-review papers (P021, P023, P025) have shown to mask the dominant failure modes (synthetic-to- real, mirrors/transparencies, weather corruption). This is not a technical bottleneck but a publication-norm one.

§11 Key research teams¶

Across the 18 ★★★ papers, two institutions hold disproportionate share of the field's headline output (C067):

  • HUST (Huazhong U of Sci & Tech) — 3/18 = 17%: P002 IGEV-Stereo (CVPR 2023), P003 IGEV++ (TPAMI 2025), P004 Selective-Stereo (CVPR 2024). The HUST cluster owns the iterative-geometry-volume route end-to-end (geometry-encoding → multi-range → frequency-aware).
  • University of Bologna (Tosi/Mattoccia group) — 3/18 = 17%: P012 TemporalStereo (IROS 2023), P017 Survey: Deep Stereo in the Twenties (preprint 2024), P021 Stereo Anywhere (CVPR 2025). Bologna owns the field-mapping + critical-review angle (taxonomy survey + failure-mode paper) and contributes the temporal-stereo lineage.

Combined: 6/18 = 33% of ★★★ output. The remaining 12 papers spread across NVIDIA Research (P005), Insta360 (P006), Wuhan U + Bologna multi- collab (P007), Fudan + Xiaomi + ShanghaiAILab + CUHK (P008), Tongji (P009), Princeton (P001), Northeastern RIVeR (P016), CityU HK + CUHK SZ + Fudan + SYSU (P022), DLUT + Tencent PCG + HKUST GZ (P024), Stuttgart + NVIDIA + Mannheim + MPI (P025), Sun Yat-sen (P019), INRIA (P020), and Beihang + Macquarie + RIKEN + UTokyo (P023).

Geographic distribution (★★★, n=18, by primary affiliation):

  • China-affiliated: ~11/18 (61%) — HUST (3), Bologna's Wuhan collab, Insta360, Fudan/CUHK clusters, CityU/Sun Yat-sen, DLUT, Tongji
  • Europe: ~5/18 (28%) — Bologna (3), Stuttgart (1), INRIA (1)
  • US: ~3/18 (17%) — NVIDIA (P005, also on P025), Princeton (P001), Northeastern (P016)
  • Japan: ~2/18 (11%) — Tokyo Tech (P026), AIST/Tsukuba (P014/P015)

Caveats: many recent papers are multi-institution and double-count; percentages are share-of-paper not headcount. NVIDIA appears as collaborator on 2 ★★★ (P005 lead, P025 collab) — the only US-industry imprint on the canonical set.

Implications for §3.5 dataset adoption: HUST's 3 papers all evaluate on the canonical KITTI/SF/Middlebury/ETH3D set; none adopt the emergent datasets (RobustSpring, MonoTrap, Mono2Stereo). Bologna papers are both adopters and producers of emergent datasets (Booster 2023+, Stereo Anywhere's failure-mode evaluations). The canonical-set inertia thus tracks the geographic split: the China-cluster majority keeps reporting on canonical benchmarks, while European labs (Bologna, Stuttgart) drive the emergent-benchmark agenda. This is a structural finding, not an aesthetic one — it predicts where the next emergent benchmarks will come from (European groups + a handful of US industry labs) and where canonical-only model claims will keep accumulating (the China-cluster lineage).

§12 Direct Q&A¶

Q: How do edge and foundation stereo matching approaches compare?

A: They occupy non-overlapping points in the design space. Foundation models (Mean 2.68 - 3.85, C015) lead in zero-shot accuracy by 2-3× over the best non-FM method (NMRF Mean 5.15). They cost 18-47M+ trainable params (C013) and 0.25-0.32s/frame on RTX 4090 (C013) — not edge-feasible without compression. Edge-deployable models (CGI-Stereo, StereoVoxelNet) trade accuracy for latency but were not benchmarked on the unified Mean metric in indexed papers.

Q: Charts for params × accuracy and speed × accuracy?

A: Generated in artifacts/. Chart 1 (params × accuracy) plots 7 of 19 models with grounded numbers. Chart 2 (Torch / proxy) plots the same 7. Chart 3 (TensorRT) is empty pending repo-grep work.

Q: Per-data-type analysis?

A: §9 above. Summary: high-frequency edges → Selective-Stereo; mirrors/transparencies → Stereo Anywhere; zero-shot generalization → Stereo Anything; speed-Pareto on RTX → IGEV-Stereo; edge deployment → no foundation model fits, use CGI-Stereo / StereoVoxelNet.

§13 Reading list and Round 3 task list¶

Entry tier (3-5 papers, suggested order, ~6h reading)¶

  1. Survey: Deep Stereo in the Twenties (P017, ~90 min) — start here for the 2020+ taxonomy and term map
  2. RAFT-Stereo (P001, ~60 min) — 2021 inflection point; recurrent field-transform paradigm that everything since 2023 inherits
  3. IGEV-Stereo (P002, ~60 min) — 2023 hybrid backbone of choice; reading this clarifies why "geometry encoding volume" matters
  4. FoundationStereo (P005, ~90 min) — 2025 foundation wave entry point; data-scale shift (1M synthetic pairs) and zero-shot framing

Deep tier (per architecture route)¶

  • Recurrent / iterative: P001 → P002 → P003 (IGEV++) → P010 (MonSter++)
  • Foundation-VFM: P005 (data scale) → P006 (frozen DepthAnything prior) → P007 (zero-shot champion) → P008 (VFM transfer)
  • Frequency / attention: P004 Selective-Stereo (frequency-aware iteration); P026 Global Occlusion-Aware Transformer
  • SSM / Mamba: P020 DenVisCoM (sole indexed paper; thin route)
  • Edge / real-time: P011 CGI-Stereo → P012 TemporalStereo → P013 Stereo Matching in Time → P016 StereoVoxelNet

Critical tier (limitations, negative results, failure-mode¶

papers)

  • P021 Stereo Anywhere (CVPR 2025) — failure-mode-targeted; only paper that explicitly sets out to break foundation-stereo
  • P023 Robust Syn-to-Real Stereo (CVPR 2024) — synthetic-to-real empirical analysis; shows where data-scale alone fails
  • P024 Mono2Stereo (CVPR 2025) — empirical study of stereo conversion; benchmark + critical analysis
  • P025 RobustSpring (ICLR 2026) — first-class corruption benchmark for stereo + scene flow + optical flow; introduces R1px metric
  • P022 PPMStereo (NeurIPS 2025) — dynamic stereo with explicit per-distance / temporal-consistency reporting

Overview tier (other surveys, for cross-checking)¶

  • P017 Tosi et al., 2024 — only deep-stereo survey within the 2020+ scope; pair with this survey for triangulation

§14 Reproducibility tier (v1.1 added)¶

This section grades each ★★★ paper's evidence provenance per the 5-question rubric in references/reproducibility-assessment.md: release / recipe / issues / quality / 3p reproduction. v1.1 covers 6 flagship papers; the remaining 12 are marked pending in paper_index.md and will land in v1.2.

Paper Release Recipe Issues Code Quality 3p Reproduced Tier
P001 RAFT-Stereo OSS complete 59 open, mixed strong (Best Student Paper 3DV 2021) confirmed (OpenStereo Model Zoo matches) R1 (C043)
P002 IGEV-Stereo OSS complete 36 open, mixed (CN-language community) strong confirmed (OpenStereo: 0.46 vs paper 0.47 SF EPE) R1 (C044)
P004 Selective-Stereo OSS complete 3 open, clean strong unverified R2 (C045)
P005 FoundationStereo OSS partial (docker-only TRT) 75 open, mixed (deployment-pain pattern) strong (NVIDIA Research) unverified R2-R3 (C046)
P006 DEFOM-Stereo OSS complete (5 train scripts) unverified strong (modular, MIT, Insta360) unverified R1 pending 3p (C047)
P021 Stereo Anywhere OSS partial (authors: "in development") 0 open, clean adequate unverified R2-R3 (C048)

Key reproducibility findings:

  1. All 6 flagships are open-source MIT/permissive — no closed-code work in the foundation-VFM family.
  2. FoundationStereo's 75 open issues skew toward deployment (Jetson, FP16 quantization, monochrome inference, point-cloud denoising). The deployment claims (6× speedup, C024) need third-party validation given this issue pattern.
  3. Stereo Anywhere is author-acknowledged "in-development" — numbers cited from this paper get an explicit caveat in §5.7.
  4. Tier-R1 papers (RAFT-Stereo, IGEV-Stereo, DEFOM-Stereo) provide complete training recipes. Tier-R2 (Selective-Stereo) is gold-standard except for missing third-party reproduction. Tier-R3 candidates (FoundationStereo, Stereo Anywhere) carry recipe / development-stage caveats.
  5. OpenStereo cross-validation (C033) provides third-party reproduction for IGEV (0.46 vs 0.47), confirming P002 numbers. No equivalent independent reproduction exists for the foundation models yet.

For citation in survey prose: every accuracy / latency claim in §4 / §6 / §8 from a Tier R3 paper now carries a "(R3 — partial recipe)" inline. Tier R1/R2 papers cite as before. The chart pipeline does not yet visualize tier; future v1.2 may add tier-based marker borders.

§10.1 Open challenges (v1.1 supplement)¶

Round 3 surfaced two new open challenges beyond v1's six:

  1. Deployment-claim reproducibility crisis: claim C024 (FoundationStereo "6× speedup with TRT FP16") and C034 (OpenStereo ships TRT pipeline with zero benchmarks) are now a 2-source pattern: the community publishes deployment pipelines but not benchmarks. Combined with the failure of arXiv abs:TensorRT queries to find any paper-side numbers, this is structural — not a survey gap.
  2. Foundation-VFM × adversarial corruption robustness untested: RobustSpring (P025) evaluated only RAFT-Stereo / ACVNet / LEAStereo / GANet (C040). No foundation-VFM model has been corruption-graded. This is a Round 4 follow-up gap.

§6.1 Cross-method speed-track (v1.1 added)¶

Regime Y axis Best example
Zero-shot Mean (chart 1 / 2) KITTI / Middlebury / ETH3D / KITTI Mean (lower better) StereoAnything 2.68 (C015)
